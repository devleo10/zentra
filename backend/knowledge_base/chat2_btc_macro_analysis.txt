### 2️⃣ Core Macro Variables You MUST Track (Non-Negotiable)

#### A. Inflation (CPI, PCE)

**Why it matters:**
- Inflation decides interest rates
- Rates decide liquidity
- Liquidity decides Bitcoin

**BTC reaction:**
- 🔼 Inflation + 🔽 Rates → BTC 🚀

#### B. Economic Growth (GDP, Jobs, PMI)

**Key idea:**
- Strong growth = Fed stays tight
- Weak growth = Fed forced to pivot

**BTC behavior:**
- Late-cycle slowdown = BTC accumulation phase
- Recession fear = volatility, but long-term bullish

#### C. US Dollar (DXY) — MOST IMPORTANT FOR BTC

**Rule:**
Bitcoin is anti-Dollar.

- 🔼 DXY → BTC struggles
- 🔽 DXY → BTC explodes

📌 Always check BTC chart against DXY, not just USD.

**Role of the Federal Reserve**

- Interest rates

- Liquidity cycles

### Fed Speech Keyword Cheat Sheet

2️⃣ **FED SPEECH KEYWORD CHEAT SHEET (ELITE EDGE)**

Markets move not on actions, but on WORDS.

All Fed members speak for the Federal Reserve.
Your job: decode tone, not emotions.

🔹 **DOVISH (BULLISH FOR BTC)**

| Fed Words | Meaning | BTC Impact |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| "Data dependent" | Flexibility | 🟢 |
| "Disinflation" | Inflation easing | 🟢 |
| "Policy is restrictive" | Rates high enough | 🟢 |
| "Balanced risks" | End of hikes | 🟢 |
| "Financial conditions tightening" | Market doing Fed's job | 🟢🟢 |
| "Tools are available" | Liquidity backstop | 🟢🟢 |

📌 When you hear 2–3 of these together → BTC accumulation phase

🔹 **HAWKISH (BEARISH FOR BTC)**

| Fed Words | Meaning | BTC Impact |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| "Higher for longer" | No cuts soon | 🔴 |
| "Inflation sticky" | Problem not solved | 🔴 |
| "Labor market strong" | No pivot | 🔴 |
| "Premature easing" | Hawkish warning | 🔴 |
| "Upside risks" | Hikes possible | 🔴🔴 |

📌 Markets fall BEFORE hikes, not after

🔹 **PIVOT SIGNAL WORDS (MOST IMPORTANT)**

| Phrase | Hidden Meaning | BTC Reaction |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| "At or near terminal rate" | Hikes done | 🚀 |
| "Lagged effects" | Policy too tight | 🚀 |
| "Monitoring credit conditions" | Stress detected | 🚀 |
| "Financial stability" | Liquidity coming | 🚀🚀 |
